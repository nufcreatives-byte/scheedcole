Hellshire Beach

Architectural & sculptural structures

The project

A seaside setting.
Designed to welcome.

Scheed Cole’s work at Hellshire Beach combines sculpture with the design of a public seaside setting. The castle-shaped entrance gives the beach a recognisable arrival point, translating the familiar form of a sandcastle into an architectural feature visitors can walk through.

The wider work included gazebos and improvements to the beach’s commercial surroundings. Carolyn Cooper documented Cole and his design team working on the Hellshire renewal in 2010, while Cole later identified the sandcastle monument among his projects in a 2014 interview.

The project shows another side of his practice: using sculptural form to shape the character of a place, alongside its everyday use by vendors, residents and visitors.
