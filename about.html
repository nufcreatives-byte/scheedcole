About Scheed Cole

Sculpting
Jamaican identity.

Scheed Omar Cole is a Jamaican sculptor, sculptural design engineer, educator and social entrepreneur whose work connects art with the development of places, industries and people.

My approach

Beyond the
sculpture.

Over more than two decades, Cole has brought together sculptural design, engineering awareness, skilled fabrication and creative problem-solving to deliver public landmarks, tourism attractions, museum environments, cultural installations and sustainable products.

Read more

His work is defined not only by how it looks, but by what it makes possible: stronger identities for destinations, memorable public spaces, new uses for discarded materials and opportunities for young people to build viable skills.

“It’s not a sculpture. It’s a message.”

Scheed Cole

His story

Rooted
in Jamaica.

Born in Montego Bay and raised in Kingston, Cole grew up learning to make the most of limited resources. What began as necessity developed into a creative approach rooted in resourcefulness, experimentation and the belief that practical solutions can also be beautiful. He studied Art Education at Mico Teachers’ College before developing a multidisciplinary practice spanning sculpture, fabrication and large-scale commissions. His sculptural and fabrication skills developed through experimentation and hands-on making.

Read more

Jamaican identity and heritage remain central to his work. Through representations of cultural figures, public landmarks and environments inspired by the island’s natural world, he seeks to honour Jamaica’s stories and demonstrate the possibilities of local creativity and craftsmanship. His work has reached beyond Jamaica, including large-scale commercial sculpture for Guyana and cultural presentations at Expo 2025 in Osaka, Japan. Across national and international commissions, his purpose remains consistent: to combine imagination with practical expertise, build with Jamaican talent, and create work whose value continues long after its installation.

Art, place & impact

Creating places.
Creating opportunity.

Explore our services

Working with municipalities, tourism bodies, developers, hospitality groups and cultural institutions, Cole approaches art as a tool for placemaking and economic development. A town entrance can become a heritage landmark. A hotel façade can become an internationally recognisable attraction. A museum can turn scientific knowledge into an immersive visitor experience. His projects show how creative investment can strengthen tourism, enrich civic identity, support local trades and give communities a renewed sense of pride.

Read more

That philosophy extends beyond physical landmarks. As founder and co-founder of 360 Recycle Manufacturing Limited, established in 2016, Cole has advanced practical circular-economy applications that transform recovered foam, plastics, paper and other waste into products, play environments and sculptural building materials. Through 360 Applied Design and its developing training and apprenticeship model, he has trained and mentored more than 100 young people, connecting creative discipline with welding, carpentry, masonry, fabrication, entrepreneurship and workplace readiness.

A collaborative practice

Built together.

Behind Scheed’s work is a dedicated core team, joined by artists, fabricators and other skilled professionals according to the needs of each project. The studio also welcomes trainees and volunteers, bringing together different experiences and skills to bring ambitious ideas to life.

Beyond the sculpture

Training the
next generation.

Teaching continues beyond the classroom. With approximately 25 years of experience teaching Visual and Industrial Arts, Cole has trained and mentored more than 100 young people through 360 Applied Design and its developing apprenticeship model. The programme combines creative practice with practical training in welding, carpentry, masonry, fabrication, entrepreneurship and workplace preparation.
