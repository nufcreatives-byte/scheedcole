The idea

Waste becomes
a conversation.

Created as part of the event's sustainability initiative, The Eco-Champion used discarded plastic bottles to make recycling visible at a larger-than-life scale.

Cole developed details including the head and facial features using threads made from plastic bottles, turning an everyday waste stream into a public artwork experienced live by runners and spectators.
