S Hotel · Montego Bay · Jamaica

Jamaica
Bobsleigh Team.

A sculptural tribute to Jamaica’s four-man bobsleigh team, displayed at S Hotel Montego Bay.

The project

A team.
A national story.

A black bobsleigh carries four helmeted figures, with green and gold detailing and Jamaica lettering across its body. The installation brings one of the country’s most widely recognised sporting stories into the hotel’s collection of cultural tributes.

S Hotels identifies its bobsleigh sculpture as a homage to the Jamaican team that made its Winter Olympic debut in Calgary in 1988. The team’s story also inspired the film Cool Runnings.

Sport and identity

Jamaica on
a different track.

The four-man team’s appearance at the 1988 Winter Olympics became an enduring part of Jamaica’s sporting identity. At S Hotel, the sculptural display celebrates that legacy alongside tributes to the country’s music, culture and athletes.

Historical context and identification of the hotel display are drawn from S Hotels Jamaica’s November 2025 press release. A DRIFT Travel hotel feature also describes the bobsleigh as a tribute to the 1988 team.
