S Hotel · Montego Bay · Jamaica

Swing A Ling

A mobile record shop and a portrait sculpture beside a sound system celebrate Jamaica’s musical culture at S Hotel Montego Bay.

The project

Music and
its people.

Painted lettering, record imagery and portraits of Jamaican musicians cover the mobile record shop. The project also includes a sculpted figure resting an arm on a sound system, bringing the people and objects of music culture into the installation.
