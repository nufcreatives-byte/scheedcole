The project

Honouring a
national legacy.

Unveiled on 12 August 2022 on the front lawn of the Hanover Parish Library, this life-size sculpture commemorates Sir Alexander Bustamante, Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and a National Hero.

The monument was funded by the Tourism Product Development Company (TPDCo) through its Spruce Up Pon De Corner Programme, supporting cultural heritage and public spaces in Lucea.

Project story

Behind
the work.

Unveiled on 12 August 2022 on the front lawn of the Hanover Parish Library in Lucea, the life-size sculpture commemorates Sir Alexander Bustamante, Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and a National Hero.

The monument was funded by the Tourism Product Development Company (TPDCo) through its Spruce Up Pon De Corner Programme, which supports cultural heritage and improvements to public spaces.

The unveiling brought together the sculptor, local officials and the Prime Minister, recognising Bustamante’s place in Jamaica’s history and his connection to Hanover.
