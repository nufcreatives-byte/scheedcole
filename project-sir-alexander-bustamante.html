The project

Honouring a
national legacy.

Unveiled on 12 August 2022 on the front lawn of the Hanover Parish Library, this life-size sculpture commemorates Sir Alexander Bustamante, Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and a National Hero.

The monument was funded by the Tourism Product Development Company (TPDCo) through its Spruce Up Pon De Corner Programme, supporting cultural heritage and public spaces in Lucea.
