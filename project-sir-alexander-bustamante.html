A life-size public sculpture commemorating Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and National Hero, unveiled at Hanover Parish Library in Lucea.

Unveiling, 12 August 2022 · Photograph: Yhomo Hutchinson / Jamaica Information Service

The project

Honouring a
national legacy.

Unveiled on 12 August 2022 on the front lawn of the Hanover Parish Library, this life-size sculpture commemorates Sir Alexander Bustamante, Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and a National Hero.

The monument was funded by the Tourism Product Development Company (TPDCo) through its Spruce Up Pon De Corner Programme, supporting cultural heritage and public spaces in Lucea.

Project details

History in
public space.

Location: Hanover Parish Library, Lucea, Hanover, Jamaica.

Unveiled: 12 August 2022.

Type: Life-size commemorative public sculpture.

Programme: TPDCo Spruce Up Pon De Corner.

The unveiling brought together the sculptor, local officials and the Prime Minister to commemorate Jamaica’s first Prime Minister and his legacy in Hanover.
