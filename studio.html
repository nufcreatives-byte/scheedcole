The team

Many hands.
One vision.

Scheed works with a core team and brings together artists, fabricators and other skilled professionals according to the needs and scale of each project. Collaborators include experienced professionals and former trainees, alongside interns and volunteers.

Wherever a project takes them, the studio seeks to work with local people and skills, building connections within the communities and countries where the work is created.
