Capabilities

What can we
create together?

Scheed Cole works with architects, developers, hotels, museums, brands, cultural institutions and private clients to turn ideas into physical experiences — from Jamaica to spaces worldwide.

01

Monumental Sculpture & Public Art

Landmarks, monuments, commemorative works, cultural figures and site-specific sculpture conceived for public and civic spaces.

02

Hotels, Resorts & Destinations

Bespoke statement sculptures and installations designed around the identity, architecture and guest experience of a place.

03

Museums & Immersive Environments

Naturalistic environments, exhibitions, themed spaces and experiential installations that turn stories into places people can enter and explore.

04

Commercial & Brand Installations

Large-scale sculptural pieces, oversized objects and memorable installations for restaurants, developments, retail environments and brands.

05

Culture & Heritage

Works that translate people, history, identity and cultural narratives into enduring physical form.

06

Bespoke Sculpture & Fabrication

One-of-a-kind work developed specifically around a client's idea, site, story and technical requirements.

The process

From an idea
to the site.

A commission can begin with a developed brief, an architectural plan or simply an idea. Scheed's practice combines artistic direction with the practical knowledge required to take ambitious work from concept through fabrication and installation.

01

Discover

Understanding the place, audience, story, ambition and practical requirements.

02

Concept & Design

Sketching, visual development, scale studies and sculptural direction.

03

Fabrication

Building, sculpting, engineering, detailing and finishing the work.

04

Installation

Coordinating the finished piece with the site and bringing the experience into place.

Materials & possibility

Built around
the idea.

Material choices respond to each commission. Scheed's experience spans conventional sculptural fabrication as well as inventive use of discarded and recycled materials including plastic bottles, cardboard, Styrofoam and paper.
