Scheed Cole

Art that moves
into the real world.

Scheed Cole is a Jamaican sculptor, visual artist, educator, trainer and fabricator. His practice sits at the intersection of art and applied design, combining sculptural sensitivity with the engineering required to realise ambitious work at scale.

From cultural portraiture to immersive museum environments and attraction-scale installations, his work is designed not simply to be viewed, but to change how a space feels and how people experience it.

Material innovation

Seeing possibility
in what others discard.

For years, Cole has explored recycling and upcycling as both a creative and practical design language. Plastic bottles, Styrofoam, cardboard, paper and other discarded materials have been transformed into sculpture, playground equipment, furniture and functional structures.

In 2025, that approach became live public art with The Eco-Champion — a 10-foot runner sculpted from plastic bottles during the Sagicor Sigma Corporate Run at Emancipation Park.

Beyond the object

Building people,
not only sculpture.

Education and training remain part of Cole's wider practice. Through 360 Applied Design, young people have gained hands-on experience in sculptural design, fabrication and the process of completing ambitious real-world projects.

The result is a practice where art, engineering, sustainability and skills development can exist together.
