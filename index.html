Scheed Cole

Art that moves
into the real world.

Scheed Cole is a Jamaican sculptor, visual artist, educator, trainer and fabricator. His practice sits at the intersection of art and applied design, combining sculptural sensitivity with the engineering required to realise ambitious work at scale.

From cultural portraiture to immersive museum environments and attraction-scale installations, his work is designed not simply to be viewed, but to change how a space feels and how people experience it.

Based in Jamaica · Available for commissions worldwide

Material innovation

Seeing possibility
in what others discard.

Cole has worked extensively with plastic bottles, cardboard, Styrofoam, paper and other discarded materials, transforming them into sculpture, playgrounds and functional objects.

In 2025, he created The Eco-Champion, a 10-foot runner made from plastic bottles during the Sagicor Sigma Corporate Run.
