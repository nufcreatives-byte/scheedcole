Scheed Cole

Art that moves
into the real world.

Scheed Cole is a Jamaican sculptor, visual artist, educator and fabricator whose practice moves between fine art, public space, attractions and applied design.

His work transforms concepts into physical experiences — from monumental figures and cultural installations to sculptural environments and objects made from unexpected materials.
