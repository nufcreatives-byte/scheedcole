A wider view.

A selection of individual works that offers a glimpse into Scheed Cole’s range, from expressive portraits and figurative sculpture to immersive event entrances. Surviving photographs record these pieces at different moments; dates and locations are included where documented.

Event entrance · Props & More archive

Tiki Mask — Digicel

An oversized sculptural mask transforms a branded entrance into an immersive setting. Scheed named a Tiki Mask entrance for Digicel at the 2007 Jazz and Blues Festival in his 2011 interview. This archive photograph was uploaded to the Props & More Flickr account in 2011; its connection to that festival edition remains unconfirmed.

Portrait relief

Bob Marley — Sculptural relief

Three expressive portraits of Bob Marley emerge from a framed relief, with locks extending beyond its edges.

Figurative metal sculpture

Mother of the Time

An open metal figure with outstretched arms explores movement, balance and the human form.

Figurative sculpture

Smile Jamaica

A smiling figure brings together fruit, birds and botanical forms in a sculptural celebration of Jamaica’s natural abundance.
